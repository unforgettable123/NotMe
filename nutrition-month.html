Nutrition Month

What is Nutrition Month?

Nutrition Month is celebrated every July in the Philippines. It promotes the importance of eating healthy foods and maintaining a balanced diet to keep our bodies strong and healthy.

Healthy Foods Examples

  • Fruits
  • Vegetables
  • Fish
  • Milk
  • Rice and other healthy grains

Importance of Good Nutrition

Good nutrition gives us energy, helps us grow, and protects our body from illnesses. Eating healthy foods and exercising regularly help us live a better life.
