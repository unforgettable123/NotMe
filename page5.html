My Favorite Sport - Basketball

Basketball is one of my favorite sports because it is exciting, challenging, and fun to play.

The game is played by two teams. Players try to score points by shooting the ball into the opponent's basket.

Basketball requires teamwork, communication, speed, balance, and good decision-making.

About Basketball

Basketball was invented by James Naismith in 1891.

The sport is usually played with five players from each team on the court.

Some important basketball skills are dribbling, passing, shooting, rebounding, and defending.

My favorite part of basketball is being able to work together with teammates and improve my skills.
