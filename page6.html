My First Love in Grade 7

A Story of Love, Memories, and Lessons

The Beginning What Happened Life Lessons

01 | The Beginning

When I was in Grade 7, I met someone who became my first love. I liked her because she was intelligent and beautiful. As time passed, I discovered that she liked me too. We started talking to each other, and eventually, we became a couple.

02 | What Happened

One day, we stopped talking to each other for several days. I became worried, so I went to her house to ask about her. Her mother told me that she was not there, so I searched for her.

Eventually, I saw her with another boy whose face I could not see at first. When I came closer, I realized that he was actually my friend. I felt hurt and disappointed, and this led to an argument between my girlfriend and me.

03 | Life Lessons

That experience taught me that love requires honesty, trust, communication, and respect. Although my first love ended painfully, it became a memory that helped me understand relationships better.

For now, I want to focus on myself, my studies, and my future. I believe that someday I will understand love better and find a relationship built on honesty and mutual respect.
